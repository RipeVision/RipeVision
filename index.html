THE CHALLENGE

One inconsistent box can compromise an entire shipment.

Visual checks are difficult to standardise at scale. Meanwhile, ripening continues after packing. RipeVision is designed to give teams another layer of information before boxes move further through the supply chain.

◉

Uneven ripeness

A bunch can contain bananas at different stages of ripeness. A box-level view can miss individual outliers without systematic inspection.

⌁

Ethylene build-up

Ethylene is associated with fruit ripening. Monitoring its presence can add useful context to a box-level quality decision.

↗

Waste and cost

Better information can help teams target intervention—such as adding ethylene absorbers to boxes flagged for further attention—instead of treating every box the same.

THE RIPEVISION MODULE

Two sensing layers.
One clearer decision.

At the heart of RipeVision is a compact inspection module. An ESP32-CAM captures images of the banana bunch and sends them to the AI ripeness-detection pipeline. A gas-sensing layer measures ethylene levels to provide an additional signal for box assessment.

  • ✓ESP32-CAM imaging
    Captures the bunch for computer-vision analysis.
  • ✓AI ripeness analysis
    Classifies bananas as ripe, unripe or overripe, identifying variation within a bunch.
  • ✓Ethylene sensing
    Measures ethylene presence as an additional indicator for the box-level workflow.
  • ✓Actionable box decision
    Flags boxes that need review or intervention before dispatch.
HOW IT WORKS

From image capture to a smarter box decision.

RipeVision brings visual ripeness classification and ethylene monitoring into one inspection workflow.

Capture

The ESP32-CAM captures an image of the banana bunch inside the inspection area.

Analyse

The AI pipeline identifies individual bananas and evaluates their ripeness levels.

Measure

The module's gas sensor checks ethylene presence and provides another input for assessment.

Decide

The system flags boxes that meet the configured acceptance rules or require intervention.

ACCEPTANCE PATH

Consistent bunch, within configured limits

The box can proceed through the workflow when the ripeness assessment and ethylene reading satisfy the configured criteria.

REVIEW / INTERVENTION

Variation or elevated ethylene reading

The box is flagged. Teams can inspect the identified ripeness outliers and consider adding ethylene absorbers where appropriate, then reassess.
