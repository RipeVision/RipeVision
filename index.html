The challenge

One inconsistent box can compromise an entire shipment.

Visual checks are difficult to standardise at scale, while ripening continues after packing. RipeVision is designed to give teams another layer of information before boxes move further through the supply chain.

Uneven ripeness

A bunch can contain bananas at different stages of ripeness. A box-level view can miss individual outliers without systematic inspection.

Ethylene build-up

Ethylene is associated with fruit ripening. Monitoring its presence can add useful context to a box-level quality decision.

Waste and cost

Better information can help teams target interventions, such as reviewing flagged boxes or considering ethylene absorbers where appropriate.

Designed for the workflow

RipeVision combines visual inspection and a gas-sensing layer to support a repeatable box-level assessment—not to replace human quality teams.

The RipeVision module

Two sensing layers. One clearer decision.

The proposed inspection module combines an ESP32-CAM image capture pipeline with ethylene sensing to add context to box acceptance and review decisions.

01 / ESP32-CAM imaging

Captures an image of the banana bunch inside the inspection area for computer-vision analysis.

02 / AI ripeness analysis

Classifies bananas as ripe, unripe or overripe, helping identify variation within a bunch.

03 / Ethylene sensing

Measures ethylene presence as an additional indicator for the box-level workflow.

04 / Actionable box decision

Flags boxes that may need review or intervention before dispatch. Final decisions remain with trained staff.

How it works

From image capture to a smarter box decision.

RipeVision brings visual ripeness classification and ethylene monitoring into one inspection workflow.

01 / Capture

Capture the bunch

02 / Analyse

Assess ripeness

03 / Measure

Check ethylene

Accept or review

FAQ

Clear answers for a changing supply chain.

What does RipeVision inspect?

Banana bunches, using camera images and AI-based ripeness classification, alongside ethylene presence as an additional box-level signal.

Does it replace quality teams?

No. The goal is to provide repeatable information and flag boxes that may need attention. People remain responsible for inspection, handling and final decisions.

What happens when a box is flagged?

Staff can review ripeness outliers and elevated readings, then consider appropriate steps under their operating procedures.

Is RipeVision commercially deployed?

Describe the current prototype and validation status accurately. Do not claim commercial validation until representative testing supports it.
